## 2. Bedrock & GDK System Paths
- GDK Minecraft Bedrock Edition (1.21.120+) stores `com.mojang` in `%AppData%\Minecraft Bedrock\users\shared\games\com.mojang`.
- Legacy UWP Bedrock stores `com.mojang` in `%LocalAppData%\Packages\Microsoft.MinecraftUWP_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalState\games\com.mojang`.
- Always ensure junctions/symlinks are mounted to both locations during Bedrock instance launch.
